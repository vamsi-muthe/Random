2. Knowledge Base Cost Model

Key Insight: 85% of KB cost comes from the OpenSearch Serverless always-on floor ($700.80/month). Variable query costs are negligible (<$10/month even at burst). You're paying for idle infrastructure.

Operational trap: Deleting a Bedrock Knowledge Base via console does NOT terminate the underlying OpenSearch Serverless collection. It continues billing $350+/month silently until manually deleted. (Ref: AWS Bedrock Agentic Retrieval blog, July 2026)
