4. Side-by-Side Comparison & Break-Even Analysis

The decisive comparison. At every projected volume, agentic search is 2.5-141x cheaper than KB.

Why this matters: This is the table you show a decision-maker. KB's cost is essentially flat ($1,900-$2,600/month regardless of usage). Agentic scales linearly from $0. The break-even point (where KB becomes cheaper) is 718+ SIAs/month - unreachable for this pipeline.

4.2 Break-Even Formula

KB becomes cheaper when:
  KB_fixed + KB_variable x N > Agentic_cost x N
  N > KB_fixed / (Agentic_cost_per_SIA - KB_variable_per_SIA)

KB_fixed = $1,902/month (OCU + maintenance)
KB_variable = ~$0.05/SIA (negligible)
